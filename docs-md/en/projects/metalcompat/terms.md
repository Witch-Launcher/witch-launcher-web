## What we provide

MetalCompat is a free, open-source (MIT) modular compatibility runtime and SDK that maximizes usable Metal functionality on legacy Apple GPUs (starting with Apple A9 / MTLGPUFamilyApple3). It is not a driver, not a replacement for Apple's Metal.framework, and not a hardware or ISA emulator.

## What we do not provide

- No GPU hardware — you supply the device
- No game files or content
- No uptime or support guarantees
- No affiliation with Apple, Khronos or Mojang
